# BASICS: everyday-play matrix (BASICS agent, 2026-11-26)

The Rift Judge suites cover edge cases thoroughly. The bug the user reported was in an ordinary situation: the first play of
the turn cost only power, and auto-pay recycled a ready rune without taking its energy first (rules-decisions #65). This
work covers ordinary play systematically. The cells are generated by crossing axes, not picked by hand, and each one is
checked against the Core Rules (docs/rules-spec.md, CR 1.5) through the public API only: `createGame`,
`getLegalActions`, `validateAction`, `applyAction` and `getView`. Board setup uses the conformance harness.

Code: `tests/basics/matrix.ts` (axes, moments, resource builders, checks), `tests/basics/oracle.ts` (an independent payment
oracle), `tests/basics/cards.ts` (small synthetic `bx-` cards), `tests/basics/driver.ts` (test grouping, known patterns).
Tests: `main-phase`, `chains`, `showdowns`, `real-cards`, `pool`, `scoring`, `manual-pay`, `oracle`, `findings` (`tests/basics/*.test.ts`).

## Checks per cell

- **Legality.** `validateAction` and `getLegalActions` agree with each other and with the rules oracle. The oracle covers
  timing (CR 307-313, 310.1.a, 159.2, 811, 144), affordability (CR 356-357, brute force), destination (CR 355.2), hidden
  turn (CR 811) and exhaust costs (CR 203.3). An illegal action must be refused by `applyAction`. The legal list has
  `endTurn` only in the Main phase, `pass` only on a chain or in a showdown, and nothing without priority or focus
  (CR 312).
- **Payment.** The end state must be the best a skilled player could reach (oracle.ts, lexicographic): first the fewest
  permanent losses (runes recycled plus Gold killed), then the most energy still available (pool + ready runes + free
  Adds), then the most power. This covers no wasted ready-rune energy, no needless recycle, the pool before runes, and free
  Adds used instead of a recycle. The cost must also be paid in full: an end state better than the oracle's best is a
  rules failure. Pool energy must be spent before a rune is exhausted just for energy.
- **Optional costs.** Accelerate, Repeat and play-trigger costs are offered if and only if they are affordable (CR 355.1,
  355.16, 383.3.b). "Pay any amount" offers exactly the maximum payable.
- **Priority and focus.** A permanent resolves at once (CR 337.2, 359.2): the turn player is back in the Main phase, or
  priority goes to the newest chain item's controller, or focus passes in a showdown (RJ#11052). A spell or ability waits
  with its controller holding priority (CR 338.1.a.5). After it resolves, the turn player is back in the Main phase, or
  the newest remaining item's controller has priority (CR 340), or focus passes and the passes start over (CR 340.2.a,
  346, 347.2.a). An Add ability uses no chain and keeps priority and focus (CR 430.2), but it restarts the passes in a
  showdown (BX-1). A move starts a showdown at an open battlefield with the mover's focus (CR 341, 345).
- **Effect.** Each card ends up where the rules put it. A unit enters exhausted unless Accelerate is paid (CR 359.2, 805).
  Gear enters ready at base (CR 149.2). A hidden card goes facedown at the battlefield and is hidden that turn (CR 811).
  Equipment attaches (CR 818). A moved unit is exhausted (CR 144). A battlefield left empty becomes uncontrolled
  (CR 190.4). A recycled rune goes to the bottom of the rune deck (CR 161.2.b). Draws happen.
- **View.** A player who isn't deciding has no legal actions and sees only a summary of the pending decision. Pool, runes
  and their states, and the chain are public. The rune deck shows only its size. Hand and facedown cards are hidden from
  the opponent and visible to their owner. This is checked before the action, while an in-step question is pending,
  after the action, on the chain, and after resolution.
- **Pool lifetime** (`pool.test.ts`, 28 cases: 4 Add sources × 7 moments). Floating resources are kept through chains,
  showdowns and the Ending step. They are emptied when a Main phase starts (CR 316.3) and at the end of each turn
  (CR 317.2.e), on either player's turn.
- **Showdown / combat endings** (`scoring.test.ts`, 13 cases). Each showdown and combat moment ends with nobody acting,
  with the winner at 0 points or at victory score − 1. Checked: control established, scored this turn (CR 472), +1 point,
  or the final point rule's draw instead (CR 473.1.b), back to the turn player's Main phase. The Beginning-phase hold
  comes after the Beginning step's chain (CR 315.2.a-b).
- **Manual payment** (`manual-pay.test.ts`, 24 groups). With `manualPay` on, a resource cost raises one `payment`
  decision with the right cost and a suggested plan. A [0] cost raises none (CR 445.1.b). The opponent sees neither the
  options nor the plan. "auto" and "manual with the suggested plan" both give exactly the auto-pay result. An empty plan,
  or one that exhausts an exhausted rune, is refused.

## Size

Default suite (`node --test tests/basics`): **14,363 cells** (6,864 of them legal plays carried out to the end) × their checks = **235,276 checks**, in 2,516 node:test tests (one per moment × kind). Separately: 28 pool-lifetime tests, 13 scoring tests, 24 manual-payment groups (7 cost shapes × 4 resource states × 6 card kinds × 4 moments), 7 oracle tests and 2 findings tests. This run uses the full resource axis on 5 representative
moments (first, after a power-only play, after floating power, the opponent's chain, own showdown) and plenty / exact /
short on the others. Views are checked on the plenty / exact / short cells. Runtime is about 45-105 s, depending on load, on the
shared 2-core box.

Full cross (`BASICS_FULL=1`, every moment × every resource state × every view check): **31,127 cells** (12,120 legal) and **976,473 checks**, about 2 min. Its only failing pattern is BX-3 (308 cells, A-1).

Everything else held across the matrix: timing in all 17 moments, affordability (no payable play hidden, no unpayable one
offered), optional costs, priority and focus after every kind of action, pool lifetime, the manual-payment flow, and
views. No rules failure other than BX-1 was found. Accelerate when only the base cost is affordable is not offered.
Hidden flips charge nothing but additional costs. The [0][C] gear (the reported case) leaves [1] floating, and the next
play spends it first. Restricted legend Adds are used only where their text allows; the resolution-time "pay any amount"
correctly doesn't use Daughter of the Void's spells-only [A]. Trigger costs were never starved by the card's own payment
in these shapes.
