## AI notes

Hand-written (kept when the report is regenerated). From the META-AUDIT card audit (2026-11-26).

**Azir (MS-4).** Every Azir card was scenario-tested with the real cards (tests/cards/meta-audit/azir.test.ts): legend
(Weaponmaster anthem, [1],[T] Sand Soldier gated on an Equipment played this turn), Azir (Accelerate, tokens from anywhere join
the attack), Guards! / Arise! (location choice, reflexive ready), Weaponmaster (choose on the chain, [C] paid, may take an
attached Equipment), all six Equipment bands, Forge of the Fluft / Hall of Legends / Seat of Power / Trifarian War Camp, and every
spell. No script or engine bug explains the winrate. Fixed on the way: Azir's token move had hint `neutral` (bots moved no
tokens); MS-5 (engine). Pilot (Azir vs Vex / Rek'Sai / Kai'Sa, 20 games per pair, seed 1): META-SIM's run 4-56 (6.7%); re-run on the audit's
pre-change snapshot 5-55 (8.3%, data/meta-sim/audit-before.json); after the audit's fixes 4-56 (6.7%, the table above;
an intermediate run 3-57, audit-after.json). Variants: Weaponmaster reducing one Power of any domain (MA-5) 3-57
(audit-exp-weaponmaster.json); 01-azir and 08-azir vs Sett / Kennen / Ezreal 15-45 (25%; audit-probe.json, 10 games per pair).

What the search AI does badly with Azir (watched games via tools/sim-lib + action logs):
- **No multi-step plans.** It is a 1-ply search with greedy settling, so "play exactly one Equipment, then the legend, then
  attach with Weaponmaster, then attack with Azir and pull the Soldiers in" is only found piecewise. Equipment played to base
  scored about the same as keeping the card, and equipping (paying [C]) scored below ending the turn, so Equipment piled up in
  hand and at base.
- **Power = runes.** Every [C] (Equip, Weaponmaster, Guards!'s ready, Accelerate) recycles a rune; the AI pays them freely and
  Azir's rune count stalls at 4-7 while opponents reach 9 (seen: 3 runes on turn 13). Raising the rune weight (1.4 -> 2.6) made it
  worse (0/24): the deck then never equips.
- **Weaponmaster churn.** The baseline bot (used in settling and as fallback) moved an already-attached Equipment to every new
  Sand Soldier, paying [C] each time (Brutalizer passed along 4 Soldiers after Arise!). Now: src/ai never moves attached
  Equipment with Weaponmaster (fastPolicy wrapper + in-step candidate filter); the engine bot still does (MA-4).
- **Never hides.** Facedown cards have no value in src/ai/eval.ts, so Guards! / Back Off / Hidden Blade (7-8 per list) are never
  hidden. Valuing a facedown card at hand value +1.8 did not help Azir (1/24).
- **Go-wide token decks under-perform in general** under this AI: unit-dense decks (Rek'Sai, Kai'Sa, Vex) flood the board
  (Rek'Sai 10-12 units / 35-40 Might by turn 12-14 vs Azir 6 / 21), and Vex's "stun every unit an opponent plays while I'm at
  a battlefield" also hits each Sand Soldier (rules-correct: tokens are played units).
- Changes made in src/ai (tests/ai green): unattached Equipment valued as a discounted promise of its Might bonus, attached
  Equipment slightly higher; rune-deck term (a recycled rune comes back next channel once the rune deck is nearly empty); optional
  in-step choices always keep "choose nothing" as a candidate; Weaponmaster filters above. Net effect on the Azir pilot: none
  measurable (5-55 / 3-57 / 1-23 in the variants tried) — the gap needs deeper search or deck-specific planning, not weights.
