# Rift Judge adjudication decisions

One decision per AMBIGUOUS / MISMATCH-RJ / OUTDATED row in docs/riftjudge/r*.md (plus rows the lead flagged). Authority:
card text (incl. errata) > official FAQs > Core Rules 1.5 > tournament rules; then house rulings, preferring (1) consistency with
CR principles, (2) consensus of several verified Rift Judge entries, (3) a single RJ entry. The same principle is applied to every
row in a group (see "Principles" at the end).

Basis keys: **OFF** = official (source + rule quoted), **HOUSE** = house ruling. Engine status: OK (test passes asserting the
decision), TODO (test asserts the decision with `todo`; the engine work is in FINDINGS.md as DECIDED-ENGINE), n/a (no observable test).

## Principles (apply to new rows the same way)
1. **Combat timing** — combat-cleanup chain items (deaths, Deathknells, responses to them) resolve before the combat result
   (CR 468.2 → 468.3); units arriving then get designations in the next cleanup (323.2); designations end at 468.7.a. (#3925, #1331, #1396, #3993)
2. **Who "played" a stolen spell** — the player who finalized it, for Legion, Battering Ram, Ravenbloom Student's controller check
   (VEN-FAQ) and for effects that marked "the next spell you play" (Ravenborn Tome). (#5287, #1393, #11710, #11755)
3. **"Kill a unit with a spell"** — the kill must be attributed to a spell you control (CR 1.5 429.5): its Kill instruction, incl. a delayed one
   it created (Noxian Guillotine, 429.5.b/d), or lethal damage it dealt right before the cleanup (429.5.c). **And you must be the player
   responsible for the kill**: for a Kill instruction, the player who performs it (your Cull the Weak on your own unit counts; the opponent
   killing theirs under your Cull doesn't). Not: another player's spell, a unit dealing the damage (Challenge, Last Breath),
   a Might reduction (Stupefy), damage marked earlier (OGN-FAQ, consistent with 429.5). (#4019, #6531, #7364, #3618)
4. **Choices are made when played** — targets, trash cards, move destinations, Repeat executions' choices (CR 355.4, 355.5, 820.2);
   costs come after (357). A "you may <verb> a <object>" in a spell still chooses the object when played (mandatory unless "up to", 355.13);
   only the decision to perform it is made on resolution (Zenith Blade). (#9227/#10433, #92a, #2722, #6669, #5998)
5. **A unit played by a resolving spell/ability enters after it finishes** (CR 354.4) — it can't see that spell's play or its later
   instructions. (#9227, #117, #6683, #3918, #12418)
6. **Prodigal Explorer and "chosen N times"** — count each enemy unit/gear chosen; Repeat executions count separately. (#2744, #92a)
7. **"One or more" triggers** — one trigger per executed instruction (a Repeat execution is a new execution); separate chain items trigger
   separately. (#6584)
8. **Self-referential Mighty** — layers per CR 478.4: a unit's own "while Mighty" Shield never keeps it Mighty. (#39, #128)
9. **Entering with a static** — not "becomes Mighty"; is Mighty for "play a Mighty unit". (#7869, #7974)
10. **"To a minimum of"** — non-passive: snapshotted when applied; passive (Leona): recomputed, decreases last. (#1347, #1448, #1405, #5249)
11. **Leading "you may"/cost on a trigger** — decided/paid at finalization (CR 383.3.a-b); incurred Deflect may be refused (PATCH-1).
12. **Hidden play effects** — targets at the battlefield played to; source gone on resolution → mistarget (CR 811.1.d.3/.3.a). (#2696)
13. **Deaths and control in one cleanup** — a dying unit's Deathknell is on the chain (pending) before the kill completes, so that
    cleanup is Closed: control and hidden cards are kept until a later Open-state cleanup (CR 808.1.d.2, 309.1, 323.5-323.6). Likewise a unit
    banished and replayed by one resolving spell (Arcane Shift) never leaves an Open moment, and any trigger met by the
    leaving (Traveling Merchant's move trigger) keeps the cleanup Closed. (#8134, #10434, #4383, #5648, #7111)
14. **One replacement, several simultaneous events** — controller of the affected objects chooses (CR 373). (#1374)
15. **Split "same location" targets** — controller picks one location; the subset kept must be maximal, no new targets (OFF since
    ADJUDICATOR-4: CR 355.11.b/b.1, Bellows Breath example). (#9125, #7422, #9125b)
16. **Copy vs Trait-Altering** — copy effects are layer 1 (CR 479.1.a); "base Might becomes" (Dragon Form) is layer 2, so it applies over
    any copy whatever the timestamps; copies take the original's printed traits (SFD-FAQ Svellsongur). (#11930, #11931)
17. **"When …" text is a trigger** — even when it reads like a passive (Stealthy Pursuer): it uses the chain and can be responded to;
    references to another object ("with it", "its location", "here") are read on resolution — gone → no effect (house, CR 383, 359.3.f);
    a trigger that refers to nothing of its source resolves normally after the source leaves (CR 393). (#2839, #8377, #11237)
18. **Who is the Attacker** — the player whose unit applied Contested, however it arrived (Charm, Bone Skewer) (CR 466.2.c.1). (#10581, #10700)
19. **"An opponent moves"** — the opponent performs the move of their own unit(s) (CR 412 responsibility). (#8279)
20. **Weaponmaster** — reduces only an [A] in the Equip cost; Equip [C]/[1] are paid in full (CR 821.1.c.3). (#10503)
21. **Relational target requirements on resolution** — the affected targets must still meet the requirement together: "up to N at the
    same location" → controller picks a location (15); an exact pair ("a friendly unit and an enemy unit at the same battlefield") → a
    lone survivor gets nothing; a single target's restriction ("a battlefield where you have units") is rechecked too — unmet → illegal, its instructions ignored. (#3003/#3021, #9125, #12510)
22. **Replaced cost actions** — a cost (incl. a kill) whose action is replaced is still paid; reductions computed from it stand
    (CR 203.2, 356-357; OGN-FAQ Cruel Patron). (#377, #1747)
23. **Play triggers vs "the next card you play"** — a trigger on playing a card fires when the play is completed (spells: by
    resolution; countered → never) and its condition is evaluated then (CR 420.4.a, 383.2.c); "the next card you play" / a card
    "being played" outside a played-check means the Play Game Action (put on the chain), discretionary or limited (420.5); "if you
    played …" checks finalization (420.4.b). Every ordinal play trigger ("your Nth card", "your first card each turn", "the first time you play a gear") counts completed plays — a
    nested Reaction that resolves first is the earlier card; non-trigger checks and cost modifiers count finalized plays. (#12212, #12025, #11762, #231, #6045, #6045c)
24. **Prevention vs "can't be dealt damage" in combat assignment** — can't be dealt damage → exempt, receives only excess; a prevention
    shield (a replacement) → not exempt, lethal computed through it; prevent-all → unreachable lethal, so it soaks the rest (CR 467.2.c.3-.5, .10). (#A-3, #6949)
25. **Move destinations** — a unit can't be moved to its current location: where the destination is chosen, the current location is
    not a valid pick (CR 355.4.a); where it is fixed ("to base", "to that battlefield"), a unit already there may still be chosen but
    doesn't move (no move triggers, no Kayn count; "when chosen" effects apply) (house, 355.9). (#1127, #124, #1492, #1767, #7817, #5384)
26. **Doubling** — adds an amount equal to the attribute's current value when the doubling happens, every applying increase and decrease
    counted (Shield, Forbidding Waste, Eclipse); a negative current value adds 0 (CR 433.1, 479.4.c). (#11368, #10971)
27. **Turn states have no phase condition** — Neutral/Showdown × Open/Closed hold "at any given time" (CR 308-310), so a staged
    showdown/combat begins at the first Neutral Open moment in any phase: Awaken/Beginning/Channel/Draw (before the remaining
    start-of-turn tasks), Main, Ending (before Expiration) (CR 323.11-323.12). (#1317, #11019, #9254)
