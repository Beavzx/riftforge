# Engine rules decisions and interpretations (ENGINE agent)

> **Source of truth.** For every ruling that came out of the Rift Judge verification (rows citing RJ#…, and any question it
> adjudicated), docs/riftjudge/DECISIONS.md (decisions + principles) is authoritative; this table records how the engine implements
> them. Where a row here and DECISIONS.md disagree, DECISIONS.md wins and the row is corrected (e.g. #29/#34 Hidden, 2026-11-26).

CR numbers are CR 1.5 (docs/rules-spec.md) unless marked "1.4" (jeff425 hyperlinked copy).
